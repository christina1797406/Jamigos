Help & FAQs

How do I select a mood and get music?

Go to the Mood Board from the sidebar, choose a mood card, and we’ll generate a playlist for that mood.

Can I preview songs before adding them?

Yes, on the Song Selection page you can listen to previews and add tracks to your playlist or create favourites.

Where can I change my settings?

You’re here! Adjust your profile, theme, and preferences from this page.

What if something isn't working?

Try refreshing the page. If the issue persists, contact jamigosdev@gmail.com
