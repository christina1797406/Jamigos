AGREEMENT TO OUR LEGAL TERMS

Jamigos is an innovative social music web app that connects with Spotify's API to let users stream music, search songs, and build custom playlists. Its core feature is a mood-based playlist generator that tailors music to users' emotions and preferences. Jamigos also includes a karaoke mode with real-time lyrics and lets users save favorite songs and karaoke moments for a personalized music experience. The platform encourages social sharing by allowing users to share playlists and karaoke moments with friends.
